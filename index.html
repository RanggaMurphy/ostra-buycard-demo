What this demo shows

The card above is Stoa's official buy card, embedded with one iframe snippet. It shows the live $OSTRA price and what a small buy gets right now on Stoa, routed across venues on Robinhood Chain.

The Buy on Stoa button opens stoadex.com in a new tab with $OSTRA selected and the referrer attached, so swaps that start here credit the referral share to the integrator's wallet. No wallet connects inside the card and nothing is signed on this page.

On the real OSTRA site, this same snippet is all that needs to be pasted where the token is presented. Nothing else on the page has to change.
